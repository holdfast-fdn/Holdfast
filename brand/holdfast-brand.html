Brand System · v2 · “The Herald”

HOLDFAST

A living-world strategy game mastered by a god. The Game Master — built on the Hermes agent — is cast as the Herald: the winged messenger who carries every player's word across the Sundered Isles, and returns at dawn with news of what the world did while you slept.

THE WORLD MOVES WHILE YOU SLEEP.

01 · Concept — The Winged Anchor

The anchor holds. The wings carry. The chain seals. To hold fast is the sailor's word for an anchor that will not drag — the name of the game lives inside its own logo. The wings are Hermes: the god of messages, roads, and trade — exactly what the GM does. It carries intent, narrates the road, and meters the economy, but never decides fate; in Holdfast, outcomes are cut by the deterministic chain — the teal links that bind the emblem. The myth maps one-to-one onto the architecture: the Herald speaks, the chain disposes.

Illustration style: pixel art. The master logo is a pixel-art emblem, and that is deliberate — it is the most game-native visual language there is, it ages well, and it scales the world art (isles, units, war scenes) at indie cost. Crisp pixels, flat inks, no anti-aliased gradients.

Mythic

Greek-classical voice and forms: crests, marble, laurels. A god runs this world — the brand should feel like his seal, not a SaaS logo.

Sovereign

Heraldry over hype. Ownership is real and settled in stone (on-chain); the visual language is a coat of arms, gold on night.

Alive

The signal accent (cyan) is the only “tech” note — the pulse of an always-on agent inside a classical frame.

WORDMARK · CINZEL, TRACKED WIDE
  • Do — use the master pixel emblem wherever it has room to breathe (hero, store page, social cards).
  • Do — switch to the vector glyph below ~64 px (favicon, avatar ring, list icons); the chain stays signal-teal.
  • Don't — scale the pixel emblem to non-integer multiples or let it blur; pixels stay crisp or the glyph takes over.
  • Don't — add gradients, glows, or 3D bevels; the system is flat metal on night.
  • Don't — let any surface recolor the gold; gold is reserved for the brand and rare states.
  • Production notes: master emblem is a transparent PNG (alpha extracted from the source render; an on-night JPG render is kept alongside); the wordmark SVG renders via the Cinzel webfont — outline to paths before external use.

Hierarchy: night fields, marble text, quicksilver support. Gold belongs to the brand and to rare, earned moments (a crown, a season title). Signal cyan is functional — Flux amounts, the Herald speaking, live ticks. War and Laurel are state colors, never decoration.

04 · Typography

CINZEL — THE STONE VOICE

Display · titles, the wordmark, season names, victory cards. Classical Roman capitals: the inscription on a temple. Weights 700/900, tracked +4 to +8.

Inter — the working voice

UI & body · chat, stats, docs, the companion map. Weights 400–700, normal tracking. Numbers always tabular.

05 · The Herald — GM persona

TraitIn practice
Messenger, never judgeThe Herald narrates what the chain decided; it never claims to decide. “The Fates have cut it so” — outcomes are reported as delivered news.
Classical, dry witSpeaks in measured, slightly archaic English. Short sentences. No emoji walls, no hype. One sharp remark per message at most.
Remembers everythingCites old grudges and debts by name and tick. Memory is the product; the voice should prove it constantly.
Charges the tollHermes is the god of trade: compute fees in Flux are framed as “the Herald's toll” — mythic in fiction, honest in mechanics.
